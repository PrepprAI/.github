# 🧠 PrepprAI

**PrepprAI** is an AI-powered mock interview platform built to help candidates master their technical interviews through realistic, dynamic, and intelligent simulations.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

To make interview preparation smarter, fairer, and more accessible by combining the power of artificial intelligence with real-world interview dynamics.

We aim to empower:
- Students preparing for placements
- Professionals switching roles or leveling up
- Bootcamp graduates entering the tech world

---

## 🌟 What We Offer

**PrepprAI** simulates a complete interview experience:
- An AI interviewer that asks both coding and behavioral questions
- A secure, browser-based environment to write and test code
- Instant feedback on your performance—code quality, approach, and communication
- A realistic pressure environment with optional anti-cheating features

Whether you're brushing up on DSA or practicing soft skills, PrepprAI provides the structure and challenge to help you grow.

---

## 💡 Why PrepprAI?

Traditional platforms focus only on problem-solving. We go beyond that by:
- Emulating real interview dynamics
- Adapting to your answers in real-time
- Encouraging communication and critical thinking
- Providing AI-generated guidance and feedback

This isn’t just prep. This is **practice with purpose**.
